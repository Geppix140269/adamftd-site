02 · Terms

Industry‑standard post‑money SAFE.

SAFE · Simple Agreement for Future Equity. Y Combinator's seed instrument. Wires of capital convert to shares automatically at the next priced round (Series A) at the lower of the valuation cap or the Series A price. No interest. No maturity. No debt. Used in the majority of US seed rounds.
