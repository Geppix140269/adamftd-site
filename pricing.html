05 · Pricing FAQ

Quick answers.

Q · Why credits, not seats?

Trade intelligence is consumed in bursts, not on eight‑hour shifts. Seat‑based pricing punishes mid‑market teams. Credits map to value delivered and produce the metric Series A investors will price on.

Q · What happens if I run out of credits?

Top‑ups are available à la carte. Or upgrade tier mid‑month, prorated.

Q · Do credits roll over?

Starter credits do not. Growth and Pro credits roll over for one billing cycle. Enterprise plans are custom.

Q · Is there an SLA?

Pro tier has priority support. Enterprise plans carry a contracted SLA with response and uptime targets, plus a named CSM.
